How a day counts. A person-day is "filed" if at least one entry exists with that due date — a separate Online Meeting and Phone call row on the same day still counts once. With the checkbox on, days before a person's first-ever entry are excluded, so a recent joiner isn't scored for a period they weren't around; days after their first entry always count, so someone who stops filing still shows up. Turn it off to see raw coverage against the full range.
